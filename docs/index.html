      FAQ

      Good questions

      Is it safe to type my real password here?

      Yes. The analysis never leaves your browser, and the leak check only sends 5 characters of a hash. You can even open the page, turn off your internet and the lab still works.

      Why does "P@ssw0rd!" score so low?

      Because swapping a for @ and o for 0 is the first trick cracking tools try. Length and real randomness beat clever substitutions every time.

      What is the fingerprint flower?

      It is drawn from the SHA-256 hash of what you typed. It cannot be reversed, but the same password always grows the same flower, so you can tell if you typed it right while the field stays hidden.

      Is a passphrase really better?

      Several random words are easy to remember and long. The key word is random: words you pick yourself are far easier to guess than words the slot machine picks for you.
